Collaborative Learning with Obfuscation Assurances for grid Knowledge (CLOAK)

Project Summary

CLOAK (Collaborative Learning with Obfuscation Assurances for grid Knowledge) is a research project to investigate whether federated learning, combined with differential privacy (DP), allows electric utilities to train shared machine-learning models on sensitive operational data without releasing that data or accepting unquantified disclosure risk.

This project is supported by the U.S. Department of Energy’s Office of Cybersecurity, Energy Security, and Emergency Response (CESER) AI-FORTS program.
